I am a researcher in the Department of Digital Technologies (DT), Leibniz Institute for Materials Engineering – IWT in Bremen, Germany. My current research focuses on using explainable machine learning (ML) models to identify process–structure–property relationships across different materials. I am developing a framework to catalog available materials datasets at IWT and use them to develop a materials science-specific LLM. Additionally, I am setting up a new high-performance computing (HPC) cluster at IWT-DT for in-house LLM training.

Previously, I was a PhD candidate at Radboud University and a Scientific Assistant at Hochschule Rhein-Waal. My research at the [Donders Sleep & Memory Lab](https://dreslerlab.org/) focused on collecting and analyzing large-scale sleep datasets using statistical and ML methods. [<i>Wearanize+</i>](https://github.com/Niloy333/Wearanize_plus), [<i>eegFloss</i>](https://github.com/Niloy333/eegFloss), and [*EEG-artifact-scorer*](https://github.com/Niloy333/EEG-artifact-scorer) are among my key outputs.
