I am a researcher in the Department of Digital Technologies (DT) at the Leibniz Institute for Materials Engineering – IWT in Bremen, Germany. My current research focus and academic work at IWT include:
- Using explainable machine learning (ML) models, especially symbolic regression with uncertainty quantification, to identify process–structure–property relationships across different materials.
- Building a catalog of available materials datasets across all IWT departments, which will be used to develop a materials science-specific large language model (LLM).
- Coordinating an institute-wide framework for publishing project protocols, datasets, and codebases in line with FAIR and open-science principles.
- Setting up a new high-performance computing (HPC) cluster at IWT-DT for in-house LLM training.
- Conducting practical sessions on the use of data analysis and ML methods in materials science and engineering.

Previously, I was a PhD candidate at Radboud University and a Scientific Assistant at Hochschule Rhein-Waal. My research at the [Donders Sleep & Memory Lab](https://dreslerlab.org/) focused on collecting and analyzing large-scale sleep datasets using statistical and ML methods. [<i>Wearanize+</i>](https://github.com/Niloy333/Wearanize_plus), [<i>eegFloss</i>](https://github.com/Niloy333/eegFloss), and [*EEG-artifact-scorer*](https://github.com/Niloy333/EEG-artifact-scorer) are among my key outputs.
