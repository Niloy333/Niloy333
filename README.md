I am a researcher in the Digitalisierungstechnik (DT) department at the Leibniz-Institut für Werkstofforientierte Technologien (IWT) in Bremen. My current research focuses on using explainable machine learning (ML) models to identify process–structure–property relationships across different materials. I am also developing a framework to catalog available materials datasets at IWT and prepare them for ML application development. Additionally, I am setting up a new high-performance computing (HPC) cluster at IWT-DT.

Previously, I was a PhD candidate at Radboud University and a Scientific Assistant at Hochschule Rhein-Waal. My research at the [Donders Sleep & Memory Lab](https://dreslerlab.org/) focused on collecting and analyzing large-scale sleep datasets using statistical and ML methods. [<i>Wearanize+</i>](https://github.com/Niloy333/Wearanize_plus) and [<i>eegFloss</i>](https://github.com/Niloy333/eegFloss) are among my key outputs.
