I am a researcher in the Digitalisierungstechnik department at the Leibniz-Institut für Werkstofforientierte Technologien (IWT) in Bremen. Previously, I was a PhD candidate at Radboud University and a Scientific Assistant at Hochschule Rhein-Waal. [<i>eegFloss</i>](https://github.com/Niloy333/eegFloss) and [<i>Wearanize+</i>](https://github.com/Niloy333/Wearanize_plus) are among my key outputs.
